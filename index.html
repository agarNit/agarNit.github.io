cat about.md

I build backend systems that hold up under real load — event-driven pipelines, streaming infra, and the occasional LLM in production that's actually monitored instead of just prompted and prayed over.

My path into this started with a data science foundation from IIT Madras, running alongside a CS degree at Chandigarh University. What stuck wasn't the math — it was watching a system either gracefully absorb 10,000 concurrent users or fall over, and wanting to be the reason it's the former.

Right now I'm at Karbon Business building agentic LLM systems for Korefi, an AI-first financial-ops platform for US restaurants — reconciliation and chargeback-dispute agents that reconcile bank, card-processor, and delivery-platform payout data, plus a pricing-intelligence agent that surfaces menu-level margin and repricing recommendations. Before that, I spent two years at Spectatr.ai: architecting a live-stream ingestion pipeline on AWS MediaLive that pushes 50+ concurrent streams to under 8 seconds of glass-to-glass latency, a Kafka backbone moving 20K+ events a day with zero data loss, and an LLM evaluation loop that took a chatbot from 32% hallucination down to 6%. Earlier, at Samsung R&D, I was debugging Android compatibility issues across 15+ device models for 500K+ active users — a good reminder that "it works on my machine" isn't a shipping strategy.

Outside of paid work, I write LLM tooling for fun — Cloudy is a from-scratch Claude Code-style coding agent I built to actually understand agentic RAG, sandboxing, and memory instead of just using someone else's.
