cat about.md

I build backend systems that hold up under real load — event-driven pipelines, streaming infra, and the occasional LLM in production that's actually monitored instead of just prompted and prayed over.

My path into this started with a data science foundation from IIT Madras, running alongside a CS degree at Chandigarh University. What stuck wasn't the math — it was watching a system either gracefully absorb 10,000 concurrent users or fall over, and wanting to be the reason it's the former.

Right now I'm at Korefi.ai, an AI-first financial-ops platform for US restaurants, where I've launched a financial-ops MVP now serving 19 brands across 37 outlets, built an AI pricing analyst that's priced 157K+ menu items against competitors, and architected "Benji" — a tool-calling QuickBooks accounting agent with human-approved writes only. Before that, I spent two years at Spectatr.ai: architecting a live-stream ingestion pipeline on AWS MediaLive that pushes 50+ concurrent streams to under 8 seconds of glass-to-glass latency, a Kafka backbone moving 20K+ events a day with zero data loss, and an LLM evaluation loop that took a chatbot from 32% hallucination down to 6%. Earlier, at Samsung R&D, I was debugging Android compatibility issues across 15+ device models for 500K+ active users — a good reminder that "it works on my machine" isn't a shipping strategy.

Outside of paid work, I write LLM tooling for fun — Cloudy is a from-scratch Claude Code-style coding agent I built to actually understand agentic RAG, sandboxing, and memory instead of just using someone else's.
